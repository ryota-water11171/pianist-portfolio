Biography

Japanese pianist Ryota Yamazaki began his piano studies at the age of seven and has become a prizewinner in major international competitions.

He received Third Prize and the Junior Jury Prize at the 64th Busoni International Piano Compettion, First Prize at the PTNA Piano Competition, and the FIrst Prize at the Thomas & Evon Cooper International Compettiion.

Ryota is currently studying at the Colburn Conservatory of Music in Los Angeles, where he studies with Fabio Bidini.
