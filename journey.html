My Journey

From the concert stage to cloud technology.

Age 7

Beginning of My Piano Journey

I began playing the piano at the age of seven. What started as a childhood interest gradually became the central part of my life.

2014

PTNA Grand Prix

At the age of fifteen, I won the Grand Prix at the PTNA Piano Competition Special Grade in Japan.

2016

Cooper Competition & The Cleveland Orchestra

I won the Thomas & Evon Cooper International Competition and had the opportunity to perform with The Cleveland Orchestra.

2019

Moving to the United States

I moved to the United States to study at the Colburn Conservatory of Music under Fabio Bidini. This marked the beginning of a new chapter in both my musical and personal life.

2023

Busoni Competition

I received Third Prize and Junior Jury Prize at the 64th Ferruccio Busoni International Piano Competition in Bolzano, Italy.

2024–2025

Orchestral Performances & Recitals

I performed with orchestras including the Yomiuri Nippon Symphony Orchestra and the Sendai Philharmonic Orchestra. I also gave recitals at venues such as Hamarikyu Asahi Hall and Munetsugu Hall.

2025

Van Cliburn International Piano Competition

I participated in the 1st Round of the 17th Van Cliburn International Piano Competition in Fort Worth, Texas.

International Chopin Piano Competition

I participated in the 1st Round of the 19th International Chopin Piano Competition in Warsaw.

2026

A New Journey into Technology

My interest expanded beyond music when I discovered a book about Python. That moment sparked my interest in information technology and led me to begin studying Linux, cloud computing, and infrastructure.

I earned the AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner and AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate certifications.

Today

Music × Technology

I am currently developing hands-on experience with Linux, SQL, Terraform, Amazon EC2, GitHub, Amazon SES, AWS Lambda, and Amazon CloudWatch.

I continue to pursue my career as a pianist while exploring a new path in technology. For me, music and technology are not separate worlds, but two different ways of learning, creating, and solving problems.
